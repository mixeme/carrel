Password recovery is not possible

Your data is encrypted with a key derived from your password. The server never stores that key, so there is nothing here that could decrypt your account on your behalf — and no reset link that would help.

An administrator can give you a new password, but doing so replaces the key: everything stored under the old one is lost. If key deposit is enabled for your account, the administrator can instead recover it with the recovery password, and your data is kept.

Ask your administrator which of the two applies to you.
